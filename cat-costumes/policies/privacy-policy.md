## Who we share it with

Shopify (hosting and checkout), Royal Mail (delivery), our payment providers, our email provider, and, if you consent to those cookies, Google, Meta and TikTok for analytics and advertising measurement. Some of these providers process data outside the UK under the UK's international data transfer rules. We never sell your data.

## Cookies

Necessary cookies keep your cart, your saved items and your cookie choice; they are stored in your browser and are not sent to us. Analytics and advertising cookies are set only after you choose to allow them in the cookie banner, and you can change that choice at any time from the link in the footer.

## Your rights

Email [CONTACT EMAIL]; we answer within one month. If you are unhappy with our answer you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
