# Refund and returns policy

Last updated [DATE].

## The short version

**30 days to send it back, worn or not.** You pay the return postage (about £3); size swaps are free.

## Our 30-day guarantee

You can send any item back within 30 days of the day it was delivered, for any reason, and get a refund. It does not matter if your cat has worn it: a cat that says no is a return, not a failure. This guarantee is on top of your legal rights, not instead of them.

## Return postage

Return postage is paid by you (about £3). Royal Mail Tracked 48 large letter is enough for a collar; a costume needs a small parcel. Keep the proof of posting until the refund arrives.

## Size swaps

If the size is wrong, tell us the size you tried and the neck measurement. Size swaps are free: we send the new size first with a prepaid label for the old one.

## How to return something

- Email [CONTACT EMAIL] with your order number and what you are sending back. We reply within one working day.

- Post it to [RETURNS ADDRESS], with your order number inside.

- We refund to the payment method you used within 14 days of the return arriving. Your bank or card provider may take a few more days to show it.

## Your legal right to cancel

Under the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013 you have 14 days from delivery to cancel an order for any reason and 14 more days to send the goods back. You pay the return postage. We may reduce the refund if the goods have been handled more than you would in a shop; our 30-day worn-or-not guarantee above is more generous than this right, and where the two overlap the guarantee applies.

## Faulty or wrong items

If something arrives faulty, damaged or not as described, the Consumer Rights Act 2015 applies: tell us within 30 days for a full refund, or later for a repair or replacement. We pay the return postage for faulty or wrong items and refund it with your refund.

## What we cannot refund

Store credit already spent, and delivery charges where you have kept part of the order. Items bought through TikTok Shop or Amazon follow that marketplace's own returns process.
