# Competitor analysis — cat costumes

Researched 14 September 2026. Catalogue data pulled from each store's public Shopify
`/products.json` feed, so product counts, prices and variant structures below are measured,
not estimated.

---

## The finding that shapes everything

**There is no large, dedicated cat-costume brand.** The category is a gap, not a crowd.
Everyone who looks like a competitor turns out to be something else:

| Looked like a rival | What the catalogue data actually shows |
|---|---|
| Happy & Polly | 177 products, median **$59.99** — but 33 cat trees, 25 fountains, 19 scratchers. Cat *furniture*, barely any apparel. |
| Fitwarm | 250 products, 1,607 variants — **zero** cat products. 101 dog shirts, 48 dog pyjamas. A dog brand with a cat landing page. |
| Meowingtons | Revenue **down 50%+ to ~$653k** (2025). Mostly merch for humans, 2 costumes in stock. |
| Comfort Style | 250 products: duvet covers, dog nappies, hair trimmers. A generic dropship junk drawer. |

That gap is the opportunity. It is also the warning: nobody has built this at scale, and the
RSPCA position on dressing cats is part of the reason.

## Your two real competitors

### 1. Clothes for Cats — the UK category owner
`clothesforcats.co.uk` · **the direct one**

- **172 products, 840 variants**, median price **£12.50**
- Ranges: 22 cat jumpers, 21 cat t-shirts, cat vests, collars, leads, fleeces
- Owns the **hairless-breed niche** — Sphynx, Devon Rex, Cornish Rex, Peterbald, Donskoy
- Free UK delivery over £40 · Klarna · Judge.me reviews · Klaviyo email · wishlist · gift cards

**Why it wins:** it sells to cats who *need* clothing. No welfare objection, no "will my cat
tolerate this", no seasonal cliff. That is a fundamentally better business than novelty.

**What its product pages do well:**
- A one-line **fit statement** — *"Loose fit. No stretch."* Instantly sets expectations.
- **Four measurements** — neck, chest, waist, back — in **both cm and inches**
- Proprietary **Size 1–5** system rather than XS–L guesswork
- Accordions: *How to measure · Care · Delivery & returns* with a real service/price table

**Weakness:** static size tables only. No tool to turn a measurement into an answer.

### 2. PIKAPIKA — the premium benchmark
`pikapikatc.com` · **the quality bar**

- **250 products**, median **$49.99** — roughly **4× Clothes for Cats**
- Deep ranges: 67 onesies, 44 vests, 38 shirts, 21 coats, 21 hoodies
- **9.8 images per product** · Judge.me *and* Loox photo reviews · rewards programme · Instagram

**Why it wins:** proof that cat apparel sustains a premium price when the imagery and
range depth justify it.

**Weakness:** Sphynx/Devon Rex focus, not costumes; little UK presence.

## What we took, and what we did better

**Adopted (they had it, we didn't):**

| Feature | Source |
|---|---|
| One-line fit statement per product | Clothes for Cats |
| Waist measurement — four measurements, not three | Clothes for Cats |
| cm **and** inches, with a live toggle | Clothes for Cats |
| Materials & care per product | Both |
| Delivery & returns table on the product page | Clothes for Cats |
| Product-page accordions | Both |
| Multi-view gallery | PIKAPIKA (9.8 imgs/product) |
| Reviews section | Both (Judge.me) |
| Wishlist | Clothes for Cats |
| Search and sort | Standard |
| FAQ page | Standard |
| Trust badges | Both |

**Where we now beat them:**

1. **Size finder.** Both publish static tables. We turn a chest measurement into an
   answer, and flag when you're at the top of a range and should size up.
2. **Breed presets.** Seven one-tap starting points for people without a tape measure.
   Neither competitor has anything like it.
3. **Informational gallery.** They show 10 photos of the same jumper. We show *where to
   measure* and *how the breakaway buckle works* — views that answer the questions that
   actually cause returns.
4. **Honesty as positioning.** Nobody in this category admits some cats won't wear
   anything. Against an RSPCA warning and 59% public disapproval, that candour is the
   only defensible stance — and it is unfair to copy.

**Deliberately not copied:** invented reviews. The reviews section renders an honest
empty state until real customers leave real feedback.

## Still open

- **Photography.** PIKAPIKA runs 9.8 images per product; we have illustrations. Unchanged
  as the #1 blocker.
- **Pricing.** Our Lion Mane is £11.99; Amazon's starts at **£3.61**. Either justify the
  gap on the page or reprice.
- **The strategic question.** Clothes for Cats proves need-based cat apparel (hairless
  breeds, recovery suits, genuinely cold cats) is the more durable business. Costume and
  novelty is the seasonal spike on top — not the foundation.
