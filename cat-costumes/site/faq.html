Questions, answered honestly

Including the ones a shop normally avoids.

Will my cat actually wear this?

Honestly? Maybe not. Plenty of cats will not wear anything, and that is a completely normal cat. Start with a bandana or a bow tie collar — they sit loosely, go on in a second and come off just as fast. If your cat tolerates that, a garment is worth trying. If they don't, we'd rather you knew before spending more.

How long can a costume stay on?

Minutes, not hours. Everything we sell is designed for short, supervised wear — a few photos, a visit, a party. Never leave a costume on a cat who is alone, and never overnight.

How do I know what size to order?

Measure the chest at its widest point, just behind the front legs, and add two fingers of slack. That single number predicts fit better than weight or breed. Our size finder turns it into a size, and every garment page carries the full chart in centimetres and inches.

What if they're between two sizes?

Size up, every time. A slightly loose costume gets worn. A slightly tight one gets fought off in ten seconds and returned.

Is it safe?

Every collar we sell uses a breakaway buckle that separates under pressure, so a snagged collar cannot trap your cat. Nothing covers the face, ears or eyes. Nothing restricts the legs. There are no wires, no hard plastic and no small parts that can come loose and be swallowed.

Do costumes calm or comfort a cat?

No, and we won't say otherwise. Calming and anxiety relief are health claims, and a costume is a costume. If your cat is stressed, speak to your vet — not a shop.

My cat hated it. Can I return it?

Yes. Thirty days, worn or not. A cat refusing to wear something is exactly the situation this policy exists for. Tell us the size you tried and we'll sort the swap.

How do I wash them?

Most garments machine wash at 30°C and should be hang dried. Hats and anything with foam or faux fur are spot clean or hand wash only. The exact instructions are on every product page under Materials & care.

When should I order for Halloween?

Earlier than you think. Pet costume buying concentrates in the two weeks before 31 October and popular sizes go first. Order by mid-October to be safe.

Do you ship outside the UK?

Not yet. UK delivery is £3.95, or free over £30.
