Frequently asked

Sizing, delivery, care and returns — answered in one place.

How do I know what size to order?

Measure the neck: wrap a soft tape where a collar sits and add two fingers of slack. The Bow Tie Collar, Pumpkin set and Santa set adjust to fit. The Lion Mane, Devil Bat Cape, Spider Costume and Bandana Collar come in sizes, and every size is a neck measurement — the full table is on the Fit & care page and on each product.

What if my cat's between two sizes?

Take the larger one. A slightly loose costume sits comfortably and photographs better; a tight one comes straight off.

Which one should I start with?

The Bow Tie Collar — it adjusts, nothing goes over the head, and it's our bestseller. The First Costume Kit pairs it with the Bandana Collar for less than buying both.

Do the hats stay on?

The Pumpkin hat has an adjustable chin strap. The Lion Mane and Santa hat fasten with velcro under the chin. None of them rely on balance.

Is anything covering the face?

No. Every piece leaves the face, eyes and ears clear. The Lion Mane frames the face; the Devil Bat Cape's hood sits behind the ears; the Spider Costume fastens under the chest.

How long does delivery take?

UK standard tracked delivery is 2–4 working days at £3.95, and free on orders over £30. The Bandana Collar ships from UK stock.

How do I wash them?

It's different for each piece — satin and faux fur are hand-wash cold, the Spider Costume is spot-clean only, the Bandana Collar goes in the machine. Every product page says exactly what to do, and there's a full table on the Fit & care page.

Can I return it?

Yes — thirty days, worn or not. Tell us the size you tried and we'll sort the swap.

When should I order for Halloween?

Earlier than you think. Pet costume buying concentrates in the two weeks before 31 October and popular sizes go first. Order by mid-October to be safe.

Do you ship outside the UK?

Not yet — UK only for now. Join the mailing list and we'll let you know when that changes.

Still stuck? Every product page has its size chart, what's in the box, materials and care, and delivery terms.
