Your legal right to cancel

Under the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013 you have 14 days from delivery to cancel an order for any reason and 14 more days to send the goods back. You pay the return postage. We may reduce the refund if the goods have been handled more than you would in a shop; our -day worn-or-not guarantee above is more generous than this right, and where the two overlap the guarantee applies.

Faulty or wrong items

If something arrives faulty, damaged or not as described, the Consumer Rights Act 2015 applies: tell us within 30 days for a full refund, or later for a repair or replacement. We pay the return postage for faulty or wrong items and refund it with your refund.
