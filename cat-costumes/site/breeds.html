Sizes by breed

Typical adult neck measurements for common breeds, and which size that points to in each of our sized pieces. Breed is a starting point — a big Bengal and a small Bengal are two sizes apart — so measure before you order.

How we got these figures

The ranges are typical adult neck circumferences we use as working figures, not a veterinary standard. The size suggestion takes the middle of the range and applies the same rule as every product page: if you're between sizes, take the larger. Use the quiz to size from your own measurement instead.
