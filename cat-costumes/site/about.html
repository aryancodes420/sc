About

Costumes made for cats, chosen by someone who has actually tried to dress one.

Catwalk Club is a small UK shop selling seven pieces we would put on our own cats: two everyday collars, four Halloween looks and a Christmas set. Every one is sized from a cat's neck, leaves the face and eyes clear, and comes with the measurements, materials and washing instructions on the page — not in a leaflet you find afterwards.

Shop the seven Say hello

🐱 Why only seven

Because seven good ones beat forty average ones. Each was picked from a supplier with a long sales record and photos on real cats, then sized, priced and written up from the listing itself. When one earns its place, we add it. When one doesn't sell, it goes.

📦 How orders work

Stock is held in the UK and dispatched tracked within one working day. Standard delivery is £3.95 and free over £30. If it isn't right, send it back within 30 days, worn or not.

📏 The sizing promise

Every size on this site is a neck measurement. If you measure, it fits. If it doesn't, the swap is on us.

📸 Your cats

Tag @catwalkclub or send us the photo. The best ones go on the site, with your permission and credit.
