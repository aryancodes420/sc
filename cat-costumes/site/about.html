About

Costumes made for cats, chosen by someone who has actually tried to dress one.

Catwalk Club is a small UK shop selling the pieces we would put on our own cats: two everyday collars, four Halloween looks and a Christmas set. Every one is sized from a cat's neck, leaves the face and eyes clear, and comes with the measurements, materials and washing instructions on the page — not in a leaflet you find afterwards.

🐱 Why so few

Because a few good ones beat forty average ones. Each was picked from a supplier with a long sales record and photos on real cats, then sized, priced and written up from the listing itself. When one earns its place, we add it. When one doesn't sell, it goes.
