🐈 Let's be honest about cats and clothes

Plenty of cats will not wear anything, and that is a completely normal cat. We'd rather say so than sell you something that ends up in a drawer.

So: everything here is built for short, supervised wear — a few photos, a visit, a party — not all day. Nothing covers the face or ears. Nothing restricts the legs. Collars break away under pressure.

If it doesn't work out, send it back within 30 days. Worn is fine. We'd rather have the costume back than a miserable cat.
