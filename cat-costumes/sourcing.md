# Sourcing research — the launch 10

Researched 14 September 2026. Every "sold" figure below was read from a live AliExpress
listing that day; every supplier claim was read from the supplier's own site. Where a
figure could only be seen behind a trade login, it says so — nothing is estimated.

---

## The four findings that matter

1. **The bow tie collar is the strongest product in the whole set.** A breakaway bow-tie
   collar with bell has **5,000+ sold at $1.09** on a single listing, with three more
   listings at 754, 487 and 343 sold. Nothing else in the ten comes close on volume, and
   nothing comes close on margin.
2. **UK-warehouse dropshipping exists for two of the ten items, not ten.** AliExpress's
   "ships from UK" filter returned genuine results only for **bandanas** and the
   **birthday bib + hat set**. For lion mane, pumpkin hat, bow tie, flower collar and
   antlers it returned the same six unrelated fallback items — meaning **no UK-stocked
   listings exist**. Those ship from China in 10–20 days, or you hold stock yourself.
3. **Two of the ten are weak and should be swapped.** The pumpkin *hat* barely sells
   on AliExpress (37–42 sold) despite pumpkin being the NRF's #1 pet costume — the
   demand is for the full pumpkin *costume*, not the hat. The dinosaur hoodie is thin
   (18–23 sold) and mostly dog-sized. A bat cape outsells the superhero cape 177 to
   almost nothing.
4. **The best UK stock source for garments is a wholesaler, not a dropshipper.**
   Prince & Princess Petwear (UK, since 2006) carries 60 cat clothing lines including a
   cable-knit Sphynx jumper, with a trade portal for online retailers. That is your
   Knitted Jumper.

### The Amazon problem, in numbers

Lion mane on Amazon UK retails from **£3.61**. Your cost floor is $4.20 (~£3.30) before
postage. You cannot price this at £11.99 and win on the same product. Either source a
visibly better one (fuller faux fur, cotton lining) and photograph the difference, or
treat the lion mane as a traffic item at ~£7.99 and make the margin on collars.

The collars are the opposite story: **$1.09 cost against an £8.99 retail** is the margin
that carries the shop.
