# Which cat costume size for your breed? Maine Coon to Siamese

Breed is the question we get asked most: *"I've got a Maine Coon — what size?"* Here's the honest answer: breed gives you a **starting point**, and a tape measure gives you the answer. A big Bengal and a small Bengal can be two sizes apart.

That said, these typical adult neck figures are what we use when someone hasn't measured yet.

| Breed | Typical adult neck | Lion Mane | Devil Bat Cape | Spider | Bandana Collar |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Kitten (4–8 months) | 18–24cm | S | S | S | S |
| Siamese / Oriental | 22–28cm | S | S | S | S |
| Domestic shorthair | 25–30cm | S | S | S | S |
| Sphynx | 24–30cm | S | S | S | S |
| Bengal | 26–32cm | M | M | S | S |
| Persian | 26–32cm | M | M | S | S |
| British Shorthair | 28–34cm | M | M | S | S |
| Ragdoll | 28–35cm | M | M | S | S |
| Norwegian Forest | 30–36cm | L | M | M | S |
| Maine Coon | 30–38cm | L | L | M | S |

The Bow Tie Collar, Pumpkin set and Santa set are one size and adjust to fit all of the above.

## Three breed-specific notes
**Maine Coons and Norwegian Forests** have a lot of ruff. Measure through the fur to the skin, and lean towards the larger size on the Lion Mane — the mane sits over their own.

**Sphynx** cats have nothing between the costume and the skin, so soft fabrics matter: the satin Devil Bat Cape and the felt Spider are the comfortable picks; the faux-fur mane can tickle.

**Flat-faced breeds** (Persians, Exotics) do best with pieces that sit back from the face — the Lion Mane frames it, the Pumpkin hat sits on top. Nothing we sell covers the face or eyes on any cat.
