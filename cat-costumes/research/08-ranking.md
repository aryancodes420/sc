## 1. The verdict in one paragraph

Nobody wins in *cat costumes* as a brand. The volume in cat costumes sits in three places: unbranded
Chinese-shipped Amazon listings (the $5.99 bat wings that sell 5,000+ units a month in the US, the
lion-mane listings with 1,500–4,000 reviews in both countries), one TikTok Shop US seller with 11.7K
sales on a bat cape, and — far behind — Made By Cleo, the only cat-first DTC brand at scale, which
makes its money on collars, bow ties and bandanas and treats costumes as four seasonal add-ons. Every
UK DTC winner (Supakit, Bells & Whiskers, Cheshire & Wain, Pipkin and Bella) sells collars, not
costumes, and won by accumulating reviews over 3–9 years, not by social reach: the best cat-wearable
TikTok in the UK has 242 followers. Giant Paws, the friend's store, sells furniture at a £109.99
median, has 2 genuine reviews and is ranked last here on evidence; it is torn down because its private
Shopify analytics are the only real conversion data Catwalk Club will get before it has its own.
The gap the ranking exposes is the one Catwalk Club is built for: a UK-stocked, cat-fitted costume
range with real-cat video, where the shopper's only current alternatives are an Amazon shelf with a
median of 26 reviews per listing (UK) or a dog costume at Pets at Home.

## 2. Method

**Scoring.** Each seller was scored 0–5 on *demand proof* (verified review counts, sold counts,
"bought in past month" badges, best-selling data), 0–5 on *relevance* (how close its cat wearables are
to Catwalk Club's seven products), 0–3 on *audience* (verified TikTok, estimated Instagram) and 0–3 on
*velocity* (new products in 2025–26, latest review date, live seasonal activity). Ties are broken by
relevance, then demand. The score is a ranking device, not a measurement; the evidence column is what
matters.

**Traffic and order estimates (ESTIMATED, one method for all).** No traffic tool (SimilarWeb, Google
Trends) was reachable from this environment (blocked in 04, 05 and 07), so traffic is derived:

| Input | Assumption | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Orders that leave a review when the store runs a post-purchase review request (Loox, Okendo, Judge.me, Trustpilot invitations) | 3–6% | industry-typical range, not fetched; every ranked DTC store runs one of these apps (VERIFIED in 03/04) |
| Store conversion rate | 1.5% base, 1.0–2.5% range | the base used in `01-maths.md` §2 (ESTIMATED there) |
| Amazon listing conversion | 8–12% | `01-maths.md` §8 (ESTIMATED there) |
| Lifetime orders | reviews ÷ review rate | arithmetic |
| Orders per month | lifetime orders ÷ months trading (only where a start date is VERIFIED) | arithmetic |
| Sessions per month | orders per month ÷ conversion rate | arithmetic |

Where a start date is not verified only the lifetime-order proxy is shown. Where Amazon shows "bought
in past month", that is the velocity figure and it is VERIFIED.

bot interstitial on every attempt (05 §0), which is why the two highest-velocity Amazon listings (#2,
#3) are not picked; the TikTok Shop US product page rendered fully.
